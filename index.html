Welcome to my Cinematic Travel Journal

I travel across Eastern Nepal documenting landscapes, trails, cultural stories, natural sounds, and the disappearing memories of our villages. This website is my attempt to preserve the beauty, silence, and spirit of the Himalayas through cinematic storytelling.
